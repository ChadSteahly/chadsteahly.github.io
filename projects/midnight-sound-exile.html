Another labor of love — Midnight. Sound. Exile. is a collection of music composed and recorded mostly late at night, once the day's chores had melted away, dreaming of sounds you might hear while walking down a city street at night: the hum of a neon sign outside the back entrance to a bar in an alleyway, the distant sound of a street performer's guitar, the silent sizzle of the city at night.
