Universe Unknown is a labor of love that took a year to write, record, and mix. The album documents a period of transition — from a 20-something journeyman to a 30-something husband and father — told through a collection of purely original songs blending homegrown synth-pop and indie-rock into a vintage, bluesy pop/rock sound. I recorded all the vocals, played every instrument, and produced the album myself.

The project wrapped with a successful Kickstarter campaign that funded mastering and distribution across major digital streaming platforms and storefronts.
