Destination

ProjectDestination
ClientVCRNOT (Band)
RoleArt Director, Graphic Designer, Illustrator
SoftwareAdobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator
Turnaround2 days

Lo-fi and vaporwave band VCRNOT asked me to design the cover for their new album, along with the imagery they'd use to market it on their website and socials.

My pitch: the album is filled with lush synthscapes, hypnotizing percussion, and retro 80s vibes — sounds that evoke hazy summer drives in Malibu and Miami Vice suits. So the concept leans into 80s-tinged styles set against tropical imagery.
